# Review checklist

Check the document in this order:

1. Purpose and audience: the intended decision, reader, and expected outcome are explicit.
2. Internal consistency: names, dates, quantities, scope, and terminology agree across sections.
3. Evidence: important claims identify their source or are clearly marked as assumptions.
4. Completeness: prerequisites, failure cases, ownership, and acceptance criteria are addressed where relevant.
5. Actionability: next steps name an owner or decision point and avoid vague verbs.
6. Readability: headings, paragraph order, and sentence length support quick comprehension.

Treat security, legal, medical, financial, and destructive operational claims as high-risk. Flag them for qualified verification instead of presenting uncertain conclusions as facts.
