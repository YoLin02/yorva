YORVA installation and data retention notice

Installing, upgrading, or repairing YORVA replaces only the application program files.

Uninstall removes YORVA program files, shortcuts, and the YORVA login-start entry. It preserves:

- YORVA settings and management data under %APPDATA%\com.yorva.desktop;
- encrypted YORVA Runtime backups;
- Hermes Runtime files, Profiles, sessions, Skills, MCP configuration, and other Hermes-owned data under %LOCALAPPDATA%\hermes;
- diagnostic bundles saved by the user.

YORVA does not provide a silent "delete all data" uninstall option. Temporary update or backup staging owned by YORVA may be removed after the related operation finishes or fails.

YORVA 安装与数据保留说明

安装、升级或修复 YORVA 只会替换应用程序文件。

卸载会移除 YORVA 程序文件、快捷方式和登录启动项，但会保留：

- %APPDATA%\com.yorva.desktop 下的 YORVA 设置与管理数据；
- 已加密的 YORVA Runtime 备份；
- %LOCALAPPDATA%\hermes 下的 Hermes Runtime、Profiles、会话、Skills、MCP 配置和其他由 Hermes 管理的数据；
- 用户另行保存的诊断包。

YORVA 不提供静默“删除全部数据”的卸载选项。YORVA 自己管理的临时升级或备份暂存文件，可在相关操作完成或失败后清理。
